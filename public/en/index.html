    Frequently asked questions

    What everyone asks before starting.

    Do I have to do anything?

    No. You tell me about your business once and that's it. I create the ideas, the designs, the copy and I post. If you want, you can review everything before it goes out.

    Does Posty post to my real Instagram account?

    Yes. You connect your Business account once and I post directly to your profile through Meta's official API. And in the free trial you already see your week ready before signing up.

    Do you use my photos and my brand?

    No need to upload anything: Posty studies your Instagram or your website and builds your identity from there — your real photos, your colors. Nothing generic.

    Can I cancel anytime?

    Yes, no questions and no hoops. Cancel from your account and you're done.

    What if I don't like a post?

    You pause it in one tap, before it goes out. Don't like something? Ask for another photo, another caption or another idea, in the chat. And I learn from what you like so every week comes out better.

    Do I have to give you my Instagram password?

    No. You connect your account with Meta's official login, just like signing in with Google on other apps. We never see your password — we never ask for it or store it.

    Do the posts really go out on their own?

    Yes, that's the idea: every morning at 9:00 you see the day's post, and 30 minutes before it goes out I send you a heads-up; if you don't say anything, it goes out. If something doesn't feel right, you pause it in one tap.

    What if it doesn't work for me?

    That's why the trial is free and card-less: chat with me, I study your business and ask only what's missing — 2 or 3 taps — and you see your week ready. If it's not for you, you pay nothing.

    When do I see my first week?

    Right away: in the free trial you chat with me — 2 or 3 taps — and you see your week ready. When you create your account, every morning at 9:00 you see the day's post, and 30 minutes before it goes out I send you a heads-up; if you don't say anything, it goes out.

    Does Posty replace a community manager?

    It does the complete everyday job: posting — with your one-tap approval when human judgment is needed. If you want to promote a post, you boost it from your own Meta account, separate from the plan. If your brand needs in-person photo or video production, or bespoke campaigns, that's still a human team's work.

    Can Posty advertise my posts?

    Yes. When a post takes off, Posty flags it and proposes boosting it: the ad spend is paid from your own Meta account, separate from the subscription, and Posty picks the post, the audience and runs it, always with your one-tap OK.

    Does Posty work on its own, or do I need to hire anything else?

    It works on its own. Posty is a complete product by itself: I come up with the ideas, design and post — all within your plan, with no need to hire anyone else or add other services. If you want to promote a post, you boost it from your own Meta account, separate from the plan. Posty is all it takes for your Instagram to be fully taken care of.
