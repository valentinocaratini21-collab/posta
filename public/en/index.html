Frequently asked questions

What everyone asks before starting.

Do I have to do anything?

No. You tell me about your business once and that's it — and if you want, you can review everything before it goes out.

Does Posty post to my real Instagram account?

Yes. You connect your Business account once and I post directly to your profile through Meta's official API.

Do you use my photos and my brand?

Yes: we work with your real photos and your colors, from your Instagram or your website. No need to upload anything.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, no questions and no hoops. Cancel from your account and you're done.

What if I don't like a post?

Something doesn't feel right? Send us a voice note and we'll fix it — another photo, another caption or another idea — before it goes out. And we learn from what you like so every week comes out better.

Do I have to give you my Instagram password?

No. You connect your account with Meta's official login, just like signing in with Google on other apps. We never see your password — we never ask for it or store it.

Do the posts really go out on their own?

Yes: every morning you see the day's post and it goes out on its own if you don't say anything; if something doesn't feel right, send us a voice note and we'll fix it.

What if it doesn't work for me?

That's why the trial is free and card-less: you see your week ready before paying, and if it's not for you, you pay nothing.

When do I see my first week?

Right away: in the free trial you see your week ready in a few taps, before signing up.

Does Posty replace a community manager?

It does the complete everyday job: posting — with your one-tap approval when human judgment is needed. In-person photo or video production and bespoke campaigns are still a human team's work.

Can Posty advertise my posts?

Yes: when a post takes off, we flag it and propose boosting it, always with your one-tap OK; the ad spend runs on your own Meta account, separate from the subscription.

Does Posty work on its own, or do I need to hire anything else?

It works on its own: ideas, design and posting are all within your plan, with no need to hire anyone else. We're all it takes for your Instagram to be fully taken care of.
