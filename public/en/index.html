Frequently asked questions

What everyone asks before starting.

Do I need to do anything?

No. You tell me about your business once and that's it. I create the ideas, the designs and the copy, and I post. If you want, you can review everything before it goes out.

Does Posty post to my real Instagram account?

Yes. You connect your Business account once and I post directly to your profile with Meta's official API. And in the free trial you already see your week built before signing up.

Do you use my photos and my brand?

Yes — that's the most important part: you upload your photos and your logo once, we define your colors, and every design goes out with your identity. Nothing generic.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, no questions and no hassle. You cancel from your account and that's it.

What if I don't like a post?

You pause it before it goes out, in one tap. Don't like the image? Redo it with another style or go back to the previous one, free. And I learn from what you like, so every week turns out better.

Does Posty also serve my customers?

With the Total plan, yes: I reply to your Instagram and WhatsApp messages with your real prices and services, I book your appointments and reservations, I follow up with anyone who asked and didn't close, and I close with a payment link or a deposit. Every conversation stays visible in your account, and anything sensitive I hand over to you.

Do I have to give you my Instagram password?

No. You connect your account with Meta's official login, just like signing in with Google on other apps. I never see or store your password.

Do posts go out on their own?

Yes, that's the idea: your week goes out on its own at the best times, without you touching anything. Before each post I give you a heads-up, and if something doesn't feel right you pause it in one tap. Prefer to approve everything by hand? You can too: turn off automatic posting in Settings and nothing goes out without your OK.

What if it doesn't work for me?

That's why the trial is free and needs no card: see your week built in 1 minute and decide. If it's not for you, you pay nothing.

When do I see my first week?

Right now: in the free trial you see it built in 1 minute, and when you create your account it starts posting on its own (with a heads-up first, in case you want to pause anything).

Does Posty reply to my Instagram comments and messages?

Yes. For every comment or message that comes in, Posty prepares the reply in your business's tone: you send it in one tap, edit it or discard it. Nothing gets answered without your approval.

Does Posty replace a community manager?

It does the complete day-to-day job: posting, replying, promoting and looking after your profile — with your one-tap approval when human judgment is needed. If your brand needs on-site photo or video production, or bespoke campaigns, that's still a human team's work.

Can Posty advertise my posts?

Yes. When a post performs really well, Posty flags it and suggests boosting it with ads. You load credit in the app, set how much you want to spend, and that's it: your best content reaches many more people.
